# Electric Vehicle Charging Analytics and Forecasting System

An Excel-based analytics application for analyzing electric vehicle charging behavior, forecasting station utilization and revenue, and supporting infrastructure-planning decisions.

## Team

* Pooja Mengaji

## Project Overview

This project uses the Kaggle **Electric Vehicle Charging Patterns** dataset to analyze EV charging behavior across station utilization, charging costs, driver patterns, battery efficiency, and seasonal demand.

The solution combines descriptive analytics, predictive modeling, dashboarding, and Monte Carlo simulation to help decision-makers understand charging-station performance, assess uncertainty in revenue and cost, and identify operational efficiency opportunities.

## Target Users

* **EV Charging Network Operators:** Forecast demand, optimize charger allocation, and plan maintenance.
* **Energy Companies and Utilities:** Estimate energy-demand variation and evaluate pricing models.
* **City Planners and Policy Makers:** Understand regional and seasonal EV charging trends.
* **EV Manufacturers and Fleet Managers:** Analyze charging efficiency and driver behavior across vehicle types.
